Ardrix privacy

Draft for review before public launch.

What we store

Your skill trees, quests, progress, calendar items and settings. Without an account, this stays on your device. With an account, it is also stored in our database so it can sync between your devices. We store your email address so you can sign in.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the service providers that run the app (database hosting and email delivery).

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, we store a notification address for your device, your time zone and the hours you chose.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You → Danger zone → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it. You can also download a copy of your data at any time from You → Download my data.

Questions? Email hello@ardrix.app.
